ANGULAR FRAMEWORK — 200 TECHNICAL INTERVIEW QUESTIONS
=====================================================

Covers classic Angular concepts plus the modern API surface (standalone
components, signals, built-in control flow, @defer, zoneless change detection,
functional guards/interceptors, SSR and hydration).
